    Why one person can slow down everyone

    When one device fills the connection (a big download, a 4K stream, or a heavy upload like a cloud backup, live stream or torrent), everyone else's data has to wait in line at the modem. Pages hang, video calls stutter and game ping spikes, even if the plan is fast. HogWatch spots those moments by watching ping, then records which device and program were busiest.

    Things that fix it

    • Turn on eero's traffic management. In the eero app, go to Settings › eero Labs › Optimize for Conferencing and Gaming. It stops one heavy user from freezing everyone else, and it's the single biggest fix for this problem.
    • Cap the big downloaders. Steam: Settings › Downloads › Limit download speed. Windows Update: Settings › Windows Update › Advanced options › Delivery Optimization › Advanced options to set limits, and turn off Allow downloads from other PCs.
    • Cap or reschedule big uploads. OneDrive, Google Drive, Backblaze and torrent apps all have an upload-limit setting. Night-time scheduling works too.
    • DirecTV: an HD stream uses about 8 Mbps and 4K about 25 Mbps per TV. Several TVs plus recordings can fill a smaller plan.
    • If a slowdown says "home network", the eero itself was struggling. Move the eeros closer together, or wire them to each other with Ethernet.
